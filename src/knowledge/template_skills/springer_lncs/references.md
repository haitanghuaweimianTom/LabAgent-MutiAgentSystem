# springer_lncs References

> 基于 arxiv 真实条目的 Springer LNCS 会议接收论文（ECIR / SGAI）。所有条目均通过 https://arxiv.org/abs/<id> 验证 comments / journal-ref 字段。

## ECIR 2024 (European Conference on Information Retrieval, LNCS)

- **arXiv:2401.01596** — *MedSumm: A Multimodal Approach to Summarizing Code-Mixed Hindi-English Clinical Queries* — Ghosh A., Acharya A., Jha P., Gaudgaul A., Majumdar R., Saha S., Chadha A., Jain R., Sinha S., Agarwal S. — **ECIR 2024 (Springer LNCS)**
  - 一句话：MMCQS 数据集（Hindi-English codemixed 医疗查询 + 视觉辅助）+ MedSumm 框架 (LLM + VLM) 生成医学摘要。
  - 验证：arxiv comments "ECIR 2024"
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01596

- **arXiv:2401.02827** — *Let's Get It Started: Fostering the Discoverability of New Releases on Deezer* — Briand L., Bontempelli T., Bendada W., Morlon M., Rigaud F., Chapus B., Bouabça T., Salha-Galvan G. — **ECIR 2024 (Springer LNCS, Industry Track)**
  - 一句话：Deezer 流媒体新发布冷启动发现的 industry talk，提出 simulation-based evaluation framework。
  - 验证：arxiv comments "Accepted for presentation as an 'Industry Talk' at the 46th European Conference on Information Retrieval (ECIR 2024)"
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02827

- **arXiv:2401.04810** — *Translate-Distill: Learning Cross-Language Dense Retrieval by Translation and Distillation* — Yang E., Lawrie D., Mayfield J., Oard D., Miller S. — **ECIR 2024 (Springer LNCS)**
  - 一句话：通过翻译 + knowledge distillation 学习 cross-language dense retrieval。
  - 验证：arxiv comments "17 pages, 1 figure, accepted at ECIR 2024"
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04810

- **arXiv:2401.05939** — *DREQ: Document Re-Ranking Using Entity-based Query Understanding* — Chatterjee S., Mackie I., Dalton J. — **ECIR 2024 (Springer LNCS)**
  - 一句话：基于 entity-based query understanding 的文档重排序模型。
  - 验证：arxiv comments "To be presented as a full paper at ECIR 2024 in Glasgpow, UK"
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05939

- **arXiv:2401.05148** — *On the Influence of Reading Sequences on Knowledge Gain during Web Search* — Gritz W., Hoppe A., Ewerth R. — **ECIR 2024 (Springer LNCS)**
  - 一句话：研究 web search 中 reading sequences 对 knowledge gain 的影响（user study）。
  - 验证：arxiv comments "Accepted at ECIR 2024"
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05148

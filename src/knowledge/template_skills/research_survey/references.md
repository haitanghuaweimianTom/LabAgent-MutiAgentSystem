## LLM 通用综述（必读类）

- **arXiv:2303.18223** — *A Survey of Large Language Models* — Wayne Xin Zhao, Kun Zhou, Junyi Li, Tianyi Tang, Xiaolei Wang, Yupeng Hou, Yingqian Min, Beichen Zhang, Junjie Zhang, Zican Dong, Yifan Du, Chen Yang, Yushuo Chen, Zhipeng Chen, Jinhao Jiang, Ruiyang Ren, Yifan Li, Xinyu Tang, Zikang Liu, Peiyu Liu, Jian-Yun Nie, Ji-Rong Wen.
  - 一句话：截至当前引用量最大的 LLM 综述（144 页、1081 引用、v19 版本），按 "pre-training / adaptation tuning / utilization / capacity evaluation" 四象限组织。
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.18223
  - 期刊版本：Frontiers of Computer Science, Volume 20, Issue 12, Article 2012627 (2026).

- **arXiv:2307.03109** — *A Survey on Evaluation of Large Language Models* — Yupeng Chang, Xu Wang, Jindong Wang, Yuan Wu, Linyi Yang, Kaijie Zhu, Hao Chen, Xiaoyuan Yi, Cunxiang Wang, Yidong Wang, Wei Ye, Yue Zhang, Yi Chang, Philip S. Yu, Qiang Yang, Xing Xie.
  - 一句话：从 "what to evaluate / where to evaluate / how to evaluate" 三维度系统梳理 LLM 评估方法。
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03109
  - 期刊版本：ACM Transactions on Intelligent Systems and Technology (TIST).

- **arXiv:2308.11432** — *A Survey on Large Language Model based Autonomous Agents* — Lei Wang, Chen Ma, Xueyang Feng, Zeyu Zhang, Hao Yang, Jingsen Zhang, Zhiyuan Chen, Jiakai Tang, Xu Chen, Yankai Lin, Wayne Xin Zhao, Zhewei Wei, Ji-Rong Wen.
  - 一句话：从"构造 → 应用 → 评估"三视角对 LLM Agent 做完整调研，提出 unified construction framework。
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11432
  - 期刊版本：Frontiers of Computer Science (FCS 2024).

## 专题 LLM 综述（用于"交叉学科启发"章节）

- **arXiv:2312.02783** — *Large Language Models on Graphs: A Comprehensive Survey* — Bowen Jin, Gang Liu, Chi Han, Meng Jiang, Heng Ji, Jiawei Han.
  - 一句话：把 LLM 与 graph 结合的场景分为 pure graphs / text-attributed graphs / text-paired graphs 三类，技术路线分为 LLM as Predictor / LLM as Encoder / LLM as Aligner 三派。
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02783
  - 期刊版本：IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE) 2024.

- **arXiv:2406.07494** — *CADS: A Systematic Literature Review on the Challenges of Abstractive Dialogue Summarization* — Frederic Kirstein, Jan Philip Wahle, Bela Gipp, Terry Ruas.
  - 一句话：系统性综述（2019-2024，1262 篇 paper），提炼 6 大挑战（language / structure / comprehension / speaker / salience / factuality），并对应到具体技术方案。
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07494
  - 期刊版本：Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research (JAIR), Vol. 82, 2025.

## Oral 论文（top 1.2% 接收）

- **arXiv:2310.15168** — *Ghost on the Shell: An Expressive Representation of General 3D Shapes* — Liu Z., Feng Y., Xiu Y., Liu W., Paull L., Black M. J., Schölkopf B. — ICLR 2024 **Oral**
  - 一句话：在 watertight 模板上定义 manifold signed distance field 实现 open/watertight mesh 的可微参数化。
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15168

- **arXiv:2306.12360** — *Protein Discovery with Discrete Walk-Jump Sampling* — Frey N. C., Berenberg D., Zadorozhny K., Kleinhenz J., Lafrance-Vanasse J., Hotzel I., Wu Y., Ra S., Bonneau R., Cho K., Loukas A., Gligorijevic V., Saremi S. — ICLR 2024 **Oral (top 1.2%)**
  - 一句话：基于 smoothed energy + one-step denoising 的离散 generative model，抗体设计中 97-100% 表达成功率。
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12360

- **arXiv:2302.02257** — *Multi-Source Diffusion Models for Simultaneous Music Generation and Separation* — Mariani G., Tallini I., Postolache E., Mancusi M., Cosmo L., Rodolà E. — ICLR 2024 **Oral**
  - 一句话：单一 diffusion 模型联合训练 music synthesis + source separation。
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.02257

## Spotlight 论文（top 5% 接收）

- **arXiv:2402.00348** — *ODICE: Revealing the Mystery of Distribution Correction Estimation via Orthogonal-gradient Update* — Mao L., Xu H., Zhang W., Zhan X. — ICLR 2024 **Spotlight**
  - 一句话：通过 orthogonal-gradient 修正 backward gradient 冲突，DICE 在 offline RL/IL 上 SOTA。
  - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.00348

- **arXiv:2310.04582** — *Universal Humanoid Motion Representations for Physics-Based Control* — Luo Z., Cao J., Merel J., Winkler A., Huang J., Kitani K., Xu W. — ICLR 2024 **Spotlight**

- **arXiv:2310.01045** — *Tool-Augmented Reward Modeling* — Li L., Chai Y., Wang S., Sun Y., Tian H., Zhang N., Wu H. — ICLR 2024 **Spotlight**

- **arXiv:2306.03346** — *Stabilizing Contrastive RL: Techniques for Robotic Goal Reaching from Offline Data* — Zheng C., Eysenbach B., Walke H., Yin P., Fang K., Salakhutdinov R., Levine S. — ICLR 2024 **Spotlight (top 5%)**
